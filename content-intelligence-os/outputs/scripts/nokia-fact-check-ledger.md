# Fact-Check Ledger — "Nokia Heard the Future and Ignored It"

Companion to `outputs/scripts/nokia-script-v1.md`. Every specific claim in the narration — number, date, quote, acquisition amount, internal memo, market share, prototype, executive claim, or research finding — is tracked here per the Scriptwriting System's fact-control rules (`brain/scriptwriting-system.md`) and now classified under the Fact Verification System (`brain/fact-verification-system.md`).

**Governing rule:** this script must NOT be recorded or published until the production gate below is CLEARED — every High-risk row Verified, cautiously verified, attributed, or rewritten; every exact number and quote tied to a source; every weak/anecdotal claim attributed or removed; human approval recorded. Approval to produce is not approval to publish unverified specifics (`workflows/episode-script-workflow.md`).

**Verification classifications (from `brain/fact-verification-system.md`):**
- **Verified** — directly supported by an Official/Academic/Major-media source.
- **Verified with caution** — supported only with a qualifier, scope limit, or as attributed analysis.
- **Needs stronger source** — plausible/useful but no source in the pack yet confirms it.
- **Use only as reported** — from a secondary or single-origin report; usable only when attributed.
- **Remove or rewrite** — high-risk and unsupported; reframe to a defensible claim or cut.
- **Safe general framing** — analytical/illustrative; asserts no specific fact.

**Risk level** = damage if the claim were wrong (legal / reputational / trust). Sources S1–S16 are defined in `outputs/scripts/nokia-source-pack.md`; buckets A–F in `outputs/scripts/nokia-verified-claims-v1.md`.
