# Fact-Check Ledger — "Nokia Heard the Future and Ignored It"

Companion to `outputs/scripts/nokia-script-v1.md`. Every specific claim in the narration — number, date, quote, acquisition amount, internal memo, market share, prototype, executive claim, or research finding — is tracked here per the Scriptwriting System's fact-control rules (`brain/scriptwriting-system.md`).

**Governing rule:** this script must NOT be recorded or published until every **High**-risk row and every **Needs external verification** row is either verified against an external source or reframed/cut per its named fallback. Approval to produce is not approval to publish unverified specifics (`workflows/episode-script-workflow.md`).

**Statuses used:**
- **Verified in project source** — supported by a source already held in the project.
- **Needs external verification** — plausible and useful; confirm against an external source before publish.
- **Remove if not verified** — High-risk and non-essential; if verification fails, cut it and use the named fallback.
- **Safe general framing** — an analytical/illustrative statement, not a specific factual claim; no single source required.

**Risk level** = damage if the claim were wrong (legal / reputational / trust).
