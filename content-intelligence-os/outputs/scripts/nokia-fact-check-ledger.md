Approval to produce is not approval to publish unverified specifics (`workflows/episode-script-workflow.md`).

**Verification classifications (from `brain/fact-verification-system.md`):**
- **Verified** — directly supported by an Official/Academic/Major-media source.
- **Verified with caution** — supported only with a qualifier, scope limit, or as attributed analysis.
- **Needs stronger source** — plausible/useful but no source in the pack yet confirms it.
- **Use only as reported** — from a secondary or single-origin report; usable only when attributed.
- **Remove or rewrite** — high-risk and unsupported; reframe to a defensible claim or cut.
- **Safe general framing** — analytical/illustrative; asserts no specific fact.

**Risk level** = damage if the claim were wrong (legal / reputational / trust). Sources S1–S16 are defined in `outputs/scripts/nokia-source-pack.md`; buckets A–F in `outputs/scripts/nokia-verified-claims-v1.md`.
