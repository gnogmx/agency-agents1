# Empire Laws — Video Format

The episode blueprint. Every Empire Laws long-form video follows this format; deviations require an "adjacent bet" slot (see `brain/content-strategy.md`) and a note in the idea scorecard.

## Specs

- **Length**: 12–18 minutes target. Long enough for documentary depth and watch-time value; short enough to maintain a premium retention curve while the channel earns trust. Re-evaluate at 30 videos.
- **Narration**: single narrator, measured delivery (~150–160 wpm), close-mic, lower register. Script style per `brain/storytelling-rules.md`.
- **Visual base**: motion-graphics documentary — animated stills, archival footage, charts, maps, and typographic moments in the house visual system (`visual-system.md`). No talking head.
- **Music**: continuous score in 3–4 movements matching act structure; silence as a deliberate device before the Law Card.

## Episode Structure (the Empire Laws arc)

| Segment | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold Open** | 0:00–0:30 | Deliver the packaging promise immediately. Peak-tension image or fact, the central paradox, the implicit question. No branding, no greeting. |
| **The Stakes** | 0:30–2:00 | Scale the empire (numbers with anchors), scale the fall or rise, and plant the episode's law as a question — never yet as a statement. Ends with a loop: "to see the law they broke, you have to see what they built." |
| **Act 1 — The Build** | 2:00–6:00 | How the empire rose. Protagonist established, the pivotal mechanism shown. The fatal flaw is planted here, visible in hindsight, while the audience admires the success. |
| **The Turn** | ~midpoint | The single scene where strength becomes weakness. Signposted, slowed down, scored. The most engineered moment of the episode. |
| **Act 2 — The Consequence** | 6:00–12:00 | Compounding results of the turn — collapse, domination, or war verdict, per pillar. Re-hooks every 2–3 minutes per `brain/retention-rules.md`. |
| **The Climax** | 2–3 min before end | The decisive scene the packaging promised. Maximum detail and tension. The promise made by title and thumbnail must be unmistakably paid here or earlier. |
| **The Law Card** | ~60–90s from end | Full-screen brand moment (`visual-system.md`): the law, named and stated in ≤ 7 words. Narration makes it transferable to the viewer's world in 2–3 sentences. |
| **The Bridge** | final 20–30s | No summary, no "thanks for watching." One provocative connection to the next question ("Nokia broke this law. But one company broke the opposite law — and died faster.") + end screen to our most-retentive relevant episode. |

## Signature Elements (every episode)

1. **The Law Card** — the brand's contract with the viewer. Never skipped, never moved before the climax.
2. **The Fatal Seed** — the flaw planted in Act 1 that pays off at the Turn. Scripts without a fatal seed are returned for revision.
3. **One-Point Charts** — every data visual makes exactly one point (per `visual-system.md`).
4. **Anchored Numbers** — every large number gets a human-scale comparison.

## Subscribe/CTA Policy

One subscribe mention maximum, ≤ 8 seconds, placed directly after the Turn payoff, framed on brand value ("we extract one law from one empire, every week"). No like-begging, no notification-bell theater, no mid-roll self-promotion.

## Delivery Checklist (per episode)

- [ ] Cold open pays the packaging promise within 30 seconds
- [ ] Fatal seed planted in Act 1
- [ ] Turn lands within ±90 seconds of the midpoint
- [ ] Re-hook present in every 3-minute window
- [ ] Climax delivers the exact promise of title + thumbnail
- [ ] Law stated in ≤ 7 words, transferable beyond the company
- [ ] Bridge ends with momentum, not closure
- [ ] Runtime 12–18 minutes; delete test applied (`brain/storytelling-rules.md`)
