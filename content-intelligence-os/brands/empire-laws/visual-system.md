# Empire Laws — Visual System

The visual identity for all Empire Laws assets: thumbnails, video graphics, channel art. Global thumbnail mechanics live in `brain/thumbnail-rules.md`; this file defines how Empire Laws looks.

## Design Position

**Premium dark documentary.** Reference feel: prestige streaming documentary key art meets financial-press seriousness. The brand should look expensive at postage-stamp size. If an asset could belong to a hype-driven finance channel or a meme channel, it is off-brand.

## Color System

- **Base**: near-black charcoal (#0E0F12) and deep slate (#1A1D23). Dark bases dominate every composition.
- **Primary accent — Empire Gold** (#C9A227): used for the law text, key highlights, rising elements. Gold = empire, value, peak.
- **Signal red — Collapse Red** (#B3282D): used ONLY for decline, danger, and fall elements (crashing charts, warning states). Scarcity keeps it potent.
- **Support**: cold steel blue (#3E5C76) for analytical/neutral elements; off-white (#EDEAE2) for primary text.
- Rule: maximum two accents per composition. Gold + red together is reserved for rise-and-fall contrast moments.

## Typography

- **Display (thumbnails, episode titles, law cards)**: a high-contrast serif (e.g., Playfair Display / Freight Big class) — signals institutional, historical weight.
- **Data & captions**: a clean grotesque sans (e.g., Inter / Neue Haas class) for charts, dates, lower-thirds.
- Thumbnail text: display serif, tight tracking, gold or off-white, never more than 3 words, minimum 8% of frame height.

## Thumbnail Identity

Recurring recognizable elements (pick per concept, never all at once):
1. **The subject treatment**: central figure or object lit dramatically against dark, slight desaturation, cinematic contrast grade. Faces: readable emotion mandatory.
2. **The gold fracture**: a signature crack/fracture motif in gold running through logos, buildings, or portraits — the brand's visual shorthand for "an empire breaking its law."
3. **The chart ghost**: a faint, elegant rise-or-collapse curve integrated into the background — data as atmosphere, not decoration.
4. Composition: subject on a thirds line, one breathing zone, glance-test compliant per `brain/thumbnail-rules.md`.

## In-Video Graphic Language

- **Charts**: dark background, thin gold/steel lines, minimal gridlines, one highlighted data point per chart. Every chart makes ONE point.
- **The Law Card**: the episode's law appears full-screen at the climax of the resolution — black screen, gold serif text, 3–4 seconds, subtle score swell. This is the brand's signature moment; it appears in every episode identically.
- **Maps/timelines**: same palette; animate progression, never present static walls of information.
- **Archival material**: graded toward the house palette (lifted blacks, cooled shadows) so mixed sources feel like one film.

## Motion Rules

- Slow, deliberate camera moves on stills (2–4% scale drift). No whip-pans, no bounce easing, no glitch transitions.
- Cuts on narration beats; pattern interrupts per `brain/retention-rules.md` achieved through content changes, not gimmick transitions.

## Audio Identity

- Score: tense, minimal, orchestral-electronic hybrid. Builds toward the midpoint turn and the climax; near-silence is permitted and powerful before the Law Card.
- Narration: measured pace (~150–160 wpm), lower register, close-mic intimacy. No radio-DJ energy.
- A consistent 2-second sonic signature under the Law Card reveal — the audio logo.

## Channel Art

- Banner: black field, "EMPIRE LAWS" in gold display serif, tagline "Every empire is built on a law." One fracture motif element. Nothing else.
- Avatar: gold "EL" monogram with fracture detail on near-black — must read at 24px.

## Quality Bar

Any asset that would look out of place next to a prestige streaming documentary poster is rejected. When in doubt: darker, simpler, fewer elements, more contrast.
