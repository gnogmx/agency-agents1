# Empire Laws — Forbidden Patterns

Hard bans. Any idea, title, thumbnail, or script containing these patterns is rejected regardless of its score. Forbidden patterns exist because they damage the brand, the retention curve, or the audience the algorithm builds for us — even when they might win short-term clicks.

## Forbidden Topics & Framings

1. **Generic founder biography** ("The Steve Jobs Story"). Life stories without a mechanism or law are commodity content owned by bigger channels. A person may anchor an episode only as the protagonist of a specific decision or mechanism.
2. **Stock tips, price predictions, or "should you invest" framing.** We explain how empires work; we never advise trades. This framing attracts an audience that abandons documentary content and invites compliance risk.
3. **Hustle-culture motivation** ("Millionaire Morning Rules", "Think Like a CEO"). Poisons the premium-strategist brand and pulls the wrong suggested-feed neighbors.
4. **Unverified conspiracy framing.** "Hidden Empire" episodes (Pillar 4) must be built on documented ownership, filings, and reporting. Insinuation without evidence is banned even when it would package well.
5. **Fresh-tragedy exploitation.** No episodes built on an ongoing tragedy involving deaths or active human suffering. Collapse stories become eligible when the story is business history, not breaking grief.
6. **Pure current-events reporting.** If our angle is the same as the news cycle's, we lose to news channels on speed. News hooks are entry points to a deeper documentary, never the whole video.
7. **Crypto/get-rich scheme coverage as promotion.** Scheme collapses qualify as documentary subjects (they're empire stories); anything readable as endorsement is banned.

## Forbidden Packaging

1. Shock-face thumbnails, red-circle-and-arrow composites, emoji in thumbnails — all off the premium visual system.
2. "You Won't Believe...", "(GONE WRONG)", "?!", ALL-CAPS titles beyond one word.
3. Deceptive gaps: any title/thumbnail promising something the video doesn't deliver (per `brain/title-rules.md` rule 4). One inflated CTR is never worth the retention and trust damage.
4. Date-stamped titles for evergreen stories ("...in 2026") — they expire the asset.
5. Borrowing MrBeast-style challenge packaging codes — wrong audience contract for documentary.

## Forbidden Script Moves

1. Openings with channel branding, greetings, or "in this video" throat-clearing (per `brain/retention-rules.md`).
2. Financial advice phrasing ("you should", "this is a buy") anywhere.
3. Moralizing filler ("greed is bad") in place of an actual law — laws are strategic, specific, and non-obvious.
4. AI-sounding filler prose: "in today's fast-paced world", "delve into", "it's important to note", "the rest, as they say, is history."
5. Uncited factual claims about living people beyond what documented reporting supports — defamation risk is an existential channel risk.
6. More than one subscribe ask, or any ask before the midpoint payoff.

## Forbidden Production Shortcuts

1. Stock-footage montage filler with no informational or narrative function.
2. Text-to-speech narration or unrehearsed one-take reads — the voice IS the premium signal.
3. Charts that decorate rather than argue (violates One-Point Charts rule).
4. Recycling another channel's specific visual sequence or script structure closely enough to be recognizable — both a copyright exposure and a brand killer.

## Enforcement

- The Viral Predictor (`agents/04-viral-predictor.md`) applies this file as a hard gate: any violation = automatic rejection, logged with the violated pattern.
- The Publisher (`agents/09-publisher.md`) re-checks packaging against this file as the final pre-publish gate.
- Additions to this file come from cohort reviews: when `memory/failed-patterns.md` confirms a pattern is brand-damaging (2+ confirmations), it graduates here.
